# Guitar analysis on synthetic signals

> **These signals are synthetic.** They are Karplus–Strong plucked strings, with seeded strum spreads, velocities, ±8-cent detuning and white noise. They are **not recordings of a guitar**. The numbers say what the analysis does on idealised strings. **Nothing here is a claim about a real guitar, a real room or a phone microphone**: that measurement has not been made, and it needs recordings of the acoustic guitar under declared conditions.

This file is generated by `node tools/analysis/evaluate-guitar.ts`. Don't edit it by hand.

How the run was set up:

- **Seeds:** held out (1000 and up). The open-set thresholds (coverage ≥ 0.45, outside ≤ 0.15) were calibrated on seeds 1–15.
- **Verdict:** a reading counts when its confidence is ≥ 0.6. Below that it is **uncertain**, which is never counted as right or wrong.
- **Sample:** 25 strums per chord per noise level, at 48000 Hz.
- **Frame:** the chord frame is read 20 ms after the detected onset.

## Speaker bleed

The reference's correct chord (C) leaks into the microphone while the learner plays a wrong one (Em). The question is whether the leak can make the wrong chord read as **right**, crediting the learner with the playback. This measures one pair of chords at three levels, on synthetic strings. It doesn't show that bleed is harmless in a room, and AnyTune still asks for headphones during microphone practice.
